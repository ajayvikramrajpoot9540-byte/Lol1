📜 Terms & Conditions

1. Service Availability & Operating Hours

FlashDrop operates across Mahoba, Uttar Pradesh (PIN 210427). Standard partner shop timings are 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM IST unless customized by individual partner stores. Live shop status (Open Now or Closed) is displayed in real time on every store card.

2. 30-Minute Delivery & Late-Night Order Policy

• Active Store Hours: Orders placed while a partner shop is open are scheduled for delivery within 30 minutes from the exact order timestamp.
• Orders Near Closing Time: For orders placed within 30 minutes before a shop's closing time, the partner shop may either dispatch the order the same night or schedule it for the next working morning (Opening Time + 30 minutes).
• Closed Store Orders: Orders placed when a shop is closed are automatically queued for the next working day's opening window.

3. Product Pricing & Lowest-Rate Ranking

All prices listed on FlashDrop are inclusive of applicable taxes and are never higher than the product's printed MRP. When using text search or Camera Photo Search, available in-stock products are automatically sorted from lowest price to highest price for customer convenience.

4. Cancellations, Returns & Refunds

• Customers may inspect fresh vegetables, dairy, and packaged goods at the time of doorstep delivery.
• Damaged, expired, or incorrect items qualify for an instant doorstep replacement or 100% refund.
• Orders can be cancelled free of charge before the partner store dispatches the delivery rider.

5. Customer Privacy & Camera Search Usage

Photos captured or selected using the Search Bar Camera feature are used solely to identify matching grocery and household products in the store catalog. FlashDrop does not sell or share personal customer information or delivery addresses with third parties.
